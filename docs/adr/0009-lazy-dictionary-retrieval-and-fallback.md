# Lazy dictionary retrieval and fallback

Analysis precedes dictionary work: opening a candidate resolves its dictionary alternatives, and selecting an alternative retrieves that article, without automatically selecting even a singleton unless exact correspondence is verified. An indexed alternative alone is not a successfully retrieved article: a technical retrieval failure advances to the next eligible dictionary provider while no article for that candidate has succeeded, presenting the new provider's alternatives for learner selection. Once an article has succeeded, retain the usable partial result and report subsequent failures without consulting another provider to fill gaps; this reconciles lazy retrieval with [ADR 0001](0001-separate-analysis-and-dictionary-roles.md)'s technical-failure-only fallback and [ADR 0005](0005-preserve-uncertainty-in-dictionary-mapping.md)'s uncertainty model, as agreed in [issue #9](https://github.com/YoukouTenhouin/Tensho/issues/9).
